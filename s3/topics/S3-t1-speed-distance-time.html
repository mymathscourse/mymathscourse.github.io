What you'll learn

Week of 7 September 2026

Speed, distance, time

  • Calculating time intervals across hours
  • Calculating time intervals across days
  • Calculating time differences
  • Calculating easy times as decimals e.g. write 1 hour 15 minutes as a decimal.
  • Calculating harder times as decimals e.g. write 2 hours 18 minutes as a decimal
  • Converting decimal hours back to hours and minutes, e.g. 3.4 hours
  • Calculating SDT using easier decimal times e.g. calculate the speed for time of 1 hour 30, distance of 60 miles
  • Calculating SDT using harder times e.g. calculate distance given time of 4 hours 17 minutes, speed of 80mph.
  • Interpreting distance–time graphs
  • Planning a schedule/journey, working back from a deadline or arrival time
Curriculum links (Experiences and Outcomes)
  • MNU 4-10b I can use the link between time, speed and distance to carry out related calculations.
  • MNU 4-10a I can research, compare and contrast aspects of time and time management as they impact on me.
