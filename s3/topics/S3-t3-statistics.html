Statistics

Curriculum links (Experiences and Outcomes)
  • MTH 4-20b In order to compare numerical information in real-life contexts, I can find the mean, median, mode and range of sets of numbers, decide which type of average is most appropriate to use and discuss how using an alternative type of average could be misleading.

Statistics (continued)

Curriculum links (Experiences and Outcomes)
  • MNU 4-20a I can evaluate and interpret raw and graphical data using a variety of methods, comment on relationships I observe within the data and communicate my findings to others.

Week of 1 February 2027

Statistics (continued)

  • Scattergraphs, correlation and line of best fit
  • Choosing an appropriate display for discrete, continuous or grouped data
Curriculum links (Experiences and Outcomes)
  • MTH 4-21a I can select appropriately from a wide range of tables, charts, diagrams and graphs when displaying discrete, continuous or grouped data, clearly communicating the significant features of the data.
