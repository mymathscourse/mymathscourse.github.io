What you'll learn

Week of 17 August 2026

Equations and Inequations

  • Solving two step equations, e.g. solve 3x − 7 = 11
  • Solving equations with unknowns on both sides, e.g. 4x − 9 = 2x + 4
  • Solving equations with brackets, e.g. 3(2x − 1) = 4x + 12
  • I can form and solve an equation from a written statement or diagram
Curriculum links (Experiences and Outcomes)
  • MTH 4-15a Having discussed the benefits of using mathematics to model real-life situations, I can construct and solve inequalities and an extended range of equations.

Week of 31 August 2026

Equations and Inequations (continued)

  • Solving equations with fractions, e.g. (2x − 1)/3 + 4x = 12
  • Solving inequations, e.g. 3x − 9 < 2(3x + 1)
  • I can form and solve inequations from a context
  • I can evaluate formulae, including negatives and powers
  • I can change the subject of a simple formula
