Areas and Volumes

What you'll learn

Week of 14 September 2026

Areas and Volumes

  • Circumference and area of a circle, including working backwards (revisit of S1 Term 3)
  • Composite areas
  • Composite areas and perimeters involving circles and semicircles
  • I can calculate arc length and sector area
Curriculum links (Experiences and Outcomes)
  • MTH 4-16b Having investigated the relationships between the radius, diameter, circumference and area of a circle, I can apply my knowledge to solve related problems.

Week of 21 September 2026

Areas and Volumes (continued)

  • Volume of a prism (area of cross-section × length)
  • Simple volumes (cylinder, cone, pyramid, sphere)
  • Non calculator skills
  • Converting between cm³, ml and litres (revisit)
Curriculum links (Experiences and Outcomes)
  • MTH 4-11c I have explored with others the practicalities of the use of 3D objects in everyday life and can solve problems involving the volume of a prism, using a formula to make related calculations when required.

Week of 28 September 2026

Areas and Volumes (continued)

  • Composite volumes (cylinder, cone, pyramid, sphere)
  • Working backwards to find a missing dimension from a volume
  • Surface area of cuboids, prisms and cylinders (light touch)
Curriculum links (Experiences and Outcomes)
  • MTH 4-11b Through investigating real-life problems involving the surface area of simple 3D shapes, I can explore ways to make the most efficient use of materials and carry out the necessary calculations to solve related problems.
