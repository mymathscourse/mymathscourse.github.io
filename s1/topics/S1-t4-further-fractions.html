Week of 12 April 2027

Further fractions

  • Prerequisite Knowledge
  • I understand how to write fractions, how to read them and what they mean (e.g. 1/5 is "one fifth" and this means we have split something into 5 equal parts and we have one of those parts.)
  • I can add and subtract fractions with the same denominator (e.g. 2/7 + 3/7 = 5/7)
  • I can simplify fractions (e.g. 12/15 = 4/5)
  • I can write equivalent fractions (e.g. 2/3 = 4/6)
  • Fractions
  • I can add fractions with different denominators (e.g. 2/3 + 4/5)
  • I can subtract fractions with different denominators (e.g. 6/5 - ¼)
Curriculum links (Experiences and Outcomes)
  • MTH 3-07b By applying my knowledge of equivalent fractions and common multiples, I can add and subtract commonly used fractions.
  • MTH 3-07c Having used practical, pictorial and written methods to develop my understanding, I can convert between whole or mixed numbers and fractions.

Week of 19 April 2027

Further fractions

  • I can convert between mixed numbers and top heavy fractions (e.g. write 2 1/3 as a top heavy fraction, write 14/5 as a mixed number.)
  • I can add and subtract mixed numbers (e.g. 1 ¾ + 2 5/6)

Week of 26 April 2027

Further Fractions

  • I can subtract mixed numbers where I need to rename a whole (e.g. 3 ¼ − 1 ⅔)
  • I can solve word problems involving adding and subtracting fractions

Week of 3 May 2027

Further Fractions

  • I can place events on a probability scale from 0 to 1
  • I can write the probability of a simple event as a fraction (e.g. rolling a 3 on a dice)
