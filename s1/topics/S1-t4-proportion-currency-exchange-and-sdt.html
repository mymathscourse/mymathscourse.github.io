Proportion, currency exchange and SDT

What you'll learn

Week of 24 May 2027

Proportion including Currency Exchange and SDT

  • I can use direct proportion to solve problems (e.g. 5 pens cost £3.50, how much do 8 cost?)
  • I can convert currencies in both directions (revisit)
  • I can calculate speed, distance or time using simple time periods (e.g. 30 minutes, 2½ hours)
Curriculum links (Experiences and Outcomes)
  • MNU 3-08a I can show how quantities that are related can be increased or decreased proportionally and apply this to solve problems in everyday contexts.
  • MNU 3-10a Using simple time periods, I can work out how long a journey will take, the speed travelled at or distance covered, using my knowledge of the link between time, speed and distance.

Week of 31 May 2027

Proportion including Currency Exchange and SDT

  • I can calculate time intervals, including from timetables
  • I can read simple distance–time graphs
  • I can recognise inverse proportion (e.g. more workers, less time)
