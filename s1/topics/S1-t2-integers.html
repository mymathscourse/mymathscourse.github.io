Week of 19 October 2026

Integers – Adding and Subtracting Negative Numbers

  • Ordering integers on a number line
  • Subtracting a number crossing zero (e.g. 4 – 9)
  • Subtracting numbers starting below zero (e.g. -3 – 1)
  • Adding to a negative number (e.g. -4 + 9)
  • Subtracting a negative number (e.g. 3 - (-5))
  • Negative numbers in context

Week of 26 October 2026

Integers – multiplying and dividing negative numbers

  • Multiplying and dividing with negative numbers, e.g. −3 × 4, −20 ÷ −5
  • Mixed calculations with negatives, e.g. −2 × 3 + 7
  • Problems in context (temperature changes, bank balances, heights above/below sea level)
