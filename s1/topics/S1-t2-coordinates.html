What you'll learn

Week of 16 November 2026

Coordinates – reading plotted coordinates and plotting given points.

  • Reading coordinates in one quadrant
  • Reading coordinates in all four quadrants
  • Reading coordinates where increments are fractional or decimal
  • Translations, reflections and rotations
  • Drawing lines of symmetry and completing symmetrical shapes on a grid
Practise this online
Curriculum links (Experiences and Outcomes)
  • MTH 3-18a I can use my knowledge of the coordinate system to plot and describe the location of a point on a grid.
  • MTH 3-19a I can illustrate the lines of symmetry for a range of 2D shapes and apply my understanding to create and complete symmetrical pictures and patterns.
