What you'll learn

Week of 4 January 2027

Fractions, Decimals, Percentages

  • I know that a fraction is a way of expressing a number which isn't a whole number.
  • I know that a fraction is a way of expressing 'how many out of a total of.....'
  • I can simplify fractions and create equivalent fractions.
  • I understand that fractions, decimals and percentages are ways of expressing the same quantity in different forms. (e.g. how do we write ¼ as a decimal and a percentage?)
  • I know by heart common equivalent fractions (those that appear on handout)
  • I can calculate FDP equivalences such as 'write 3/20 as a percentage and as a decimal'
Curriculum links (Experiences and Outcomes)
  • MNU 3-07a I can solve problems by carrying out calculations with a wide range of fractions, decimal fractions and percentages, using my answers to make comparisons and informed choices for real-life situations.

Week of 11 January 2027

Fractions, Decimals, Percentages

  • I can find 50%, 25%, 10%, 5% and 1% without a calculator and build other percentages from them
  • I can work out a percentage of an amount (both calculator and non-calculator) (e.g. find 20% of £450, find 7% of 890kg)
  • I can increase an amount by a given percentage (e.g. increase $40 by 20%)
  • I can decrease an amount by a given percentage (e.g. 15% off in a sale)

Week of 18 January 2027

Fractions, Decimals, Percentages

  • I can work out a unitary fraction of an amount (e.g. find 1/5 of 45)
  • I can work out a non-unitary fraction of an amount (e.g. find 2/7 of 49)
  • I can use my knowledge of fractions, decimals and percentages to solve problems in a real life context (e.g. a plank of wood is 128cm long. A carpenter cuts 3/8 off. What is the length of the plank now?)
  • I can compare offers to make an informed choice (e.g. 3 for the price of 2 or 30% off?)
