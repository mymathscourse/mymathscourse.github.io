Money

What you'll learn

Week of 15 March 2027

Money

  • Prerequisite knowledge
  • I can use multiplying relationships and apply these to tables to find missing numbers.
  • I can multiply decimals (using the lattice method or another strategy)
  • I can round numbers to 1, 2 and 3 decimal places.
  • I can calculate percentage increases.
  • Money
  • I can change money from pounds into other currencies with and without a calculator
  • I can change money from other currencies back into pounds with and without a calculator.
  • I can work out a 'hire purchase' cost where there is a deposit and instalments.
  • I can work out the interest earned on money in a bank account.
  • I can find the best savings rates
Curriculum links (Experiences and Outcomes)
  • MNU 3-09a When considering how to spend my money, I can source, compare and contrast different contracts and services, discuss their advantages and disadvantages, and explain which offer best value to me.

Week of 22 March 2027

Money

  • I can compare contracts and services (e.g. mobile phones, streaming) and explain which is best value for me
  • I can plan a budget for an event or trip, using a spreadsheet
  • I can calculate profit and loss
Curriculum links (Experiences and Outcomes)
  • MNU 3-09b I can budget effectively, making use of technology and other methods, to manage money and plan for future expenses.
