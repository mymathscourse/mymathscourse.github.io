Circle

What you'll learn

Week of 1 March 2027

Circle 1 (including rounding)

  • Prerequisite knowledge
  • I can round to 1, 2 and 3 decimal places.
  • I can use an appropriate strategy to multiply numbers involving decimals.
  • I can use appropriate units for lengths.
  • Circle Work
  • I can label the chord, radius, diameter and circumference on a diagram of a circle.
  • I can find the radius of a circle when given the diameter.
  • I can find the diameter of a circle when given the radius.
  • I know that the formula for the circumference is given by C = pi x diameter.
  • I know that pi is an irrational number
  • I know that pi = 3.14 to 2 decimal places.
  • I can calculate the circumference of a circle and round my answer sensibly
  • I can use my knowledge of circles and perimeter to find the length of arcs and the perimeter of shapes involving circles or parts of circles.
Curriculum links (Experiences and Outcomes)
  • MTH 4-16b Having investigated the relationships between the radius, diameter, circumference and area of a circle, I can apply my knowledge to solve related problems.
  • MNU 3-01a I can round a number using an appropriate degree of accuracy, having taken into account the context of the problem.

Week of 8 March 2027

Circle 2

  • I can calculate the area of a circle using A = πr²
  • I can find the area of semicircles, quarter circles and composite shapes involving circles
  • I can work backwards to find the diameter from the circumference
  • I can solve problems in context (e.g. which pizza is better value?)
