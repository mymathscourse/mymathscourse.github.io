Volume

What you'll learn

Week of 8 February 2027

Volume

  • I can find volume by counting cubes and understand cm³ and m³
  • I can calculate the volume of a cube and cuboid using V = l × b × h
  • I can convert between cm³, ml and litres (1 cm³ = 1 ml, 1000 cm³ = 1 litre)
  • I can find a missing dimension when given the volume
Curriculum links (Experiences and Outcomes)
  • MNU 3-11a I can solve practical problems by applying my knowledge of measure, choosing the appropriate units and degree of accuracy for the task and using a formula to calculate area or volume when required.

Week of 22 February 2027

Volume

  • I can find the volume of composite shapes made from cuboids
  • I can find the volume of a triangular prism (area of cross-section × length)
  • I can solve practical problems (e.g. how many boxes fit in a container? how long to fill a tank?)
Curriculum links (Experiences and Outcomes)
  • MTH 3-11b Having investigated different routes to a solution, I can find the area of compound 2D shapes and the volume of compound 3D objects, applying my knowledge to solve practical problems.
