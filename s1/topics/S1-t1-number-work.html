What you'll learn

Week of 10 August 2026

Number Work – multiplying and dividing by multiples of 10, 100, 0.1, 0.01

  • Times tables facts (ongoing learning and review on TTRS)
  • Multiplying whole numbers by ten, one hundred, one thousand, with and then without place value chart (e.g. 44 x 10, 34 x 100)
  • Multiplying decimal numbers by ten with and then without a place value chart. (e.g. 2.9 x 100, 45.7 x 1000)
  • Dividing whole numbers by 10, 100, 1000 with and then without a place value chart (e.g. 72/10, 452/100)
  • Dividing decimal numbers by 10, 100, 1000 with and then without a place value chart (e.g. 7.3 / 10, 45.32/100)
  • Multiplying multiples of 10, 100, 1000 (e.g. 60 x 70)
  • Multiplying multiples of 0.1, 0.01, 0.001 (e.g. 0.4 x 0.6)
  • Dividing by multiples of 10, 100, 1000 (720 / 90)
  • Dividing by multiples of 0.1, 0.01, 0.001 (e.g. 4.8 / 6)
Practise this online
Curriculum links (Experiences and Outcomes)
  • MNU 3-03b I can continue to recall number facts quickly and use them accurately when making calculations.

Week of 17 August 2026

Number work – lattice method multiplying (including with decimals)

  • Times tables facts (ongoing use of TTRS to build fluency, and strategies to support if fluency is difficult to achieve).
  • Complete lattice multiplication where the grid is drawn and the numbers already placed (e.g. 46 x 7, 38 x 25)
  • Complete lattice multiplication where you have to draw the grid yourself (e.g. 52 x 78)
  • Complete lattice multiplication using decimals (e.g. 3.14 x 18 – familiarity with multiplying by pi is a helpful skill as preparation for circle topics)
  • Use multiplication skills to complete word problem (e.g. S1 are selling cupcakes to raise money for Crossroads. They price their cupcakes at £1.75 and sell 19 – how much money do they make. Ext: calculate the costs of ingredients to make 50 cupcakes using a basic recipe. If cupcakes sell at £1.75, what would the profit be given the costs of the ingredients?)
  • Use multiplication skills to understand patterns (Don Steward worksheets)
Curriculum links (Experiences and Outcomes)
  • MNU 3-03a I can use a variety of methods to solve number problems in familiar contexts, clearly communicating my processes and solutions.

Week of 24 August 2026

Number work – estimation and multiplication problems

  • I can use BODMAS in multi-step calculations (recap from transition)
  • I can estimate an answer before multiplying and use it to check my answer
  • I can solve multi-step word problems, choosing the operations needed
Curriculum links (Experiences and Outcomes)
  • MNU 3-03a I can use a variety of methods to solve number problems in familiar contexts, clearly communicating my processes and solutions.

Week of 31 August 2026

Number work – adding and subtracting decimals

  • I can add and subtract decimals mentally, e.g. 0.6 + 0.7, 5 − 0.35
  • I can add decimals in columns, lining up the decimal point, e.g. 12.6 + 3.85
  • I can subtract decimals with different numbers of decimal places, e.g. 12 − 3.47
  • I can solve money and measurement problems with decimals and check by estimating
Curriculum links (Experiences and Outcomes)
  • MNU 3-03a I can use a variety of methods to solve number problems in familiar contexts, clearly communicating my processes and solutions.

Week of 7 September 2026

Number work – bus stop division (including with decimals)

  • Prior knowledge – bus stop division where there is no remainder, and where the quotient and remainder are both given
  • Times tables facts, fluency in division facts
  • Division using the bus stop method where the answer results in a decimal (using place value counters) (e.g. 698 / 8)
  • Division using the bus stop method where the answer results in a recurring decimal. (e.g. 687 / 9)
  • Division using the bus stop method resulting in a decimal that needs rounding (e.g. 439 / 7)
  • Division word problems, including deciding what to do with a remainder
Curriculum links (Experiences and Outcomes)
  • MNU 3-03a I can use a variety of methods to solve number problems in familiar contexts, clearly communicating my processes and solutions.
