Week of 21 September 2026

Measurement and Angles – units, converting and angle facts

  • I can choose sensible units for length, mass and capacity
  • I can read scales on measuring instruments
  • I can convert between metric units (mm, cm, m, km; g, kg; ml, l) by multiplying and dividing by 10, 100, 1000
  • I can solve problems involving mixed units
  • Naming and classifying angles (acute, right, obtuse, straight, reflex)
  • Angles on a straight line add to 180° and angles round a point add to 360°
  • Angles in a triangle add to 180°, including isosceles triangles
  • Vertically opposite angles and angles in parallel lines
  • Combining angle facts to find missing angles, giving a reason for each step
Curriculum links (Experiences and Outcomes)
  • MNU 3-11a I can solve practical problems by applying my knowledge of measure, choosing the appropriate units and degree of accuracy for the task and using a formula to calculate area or volume when required.
  • MTH 3-17a I can name angles and find their sizes using my knowledge of the properties of a range of 2D shapes and the angle properties associated with intersecting and parallel lines.
