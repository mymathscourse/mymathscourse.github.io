Algebra

What you'll learn

Week of 23 November 2026

Algebra – collecting like terms, simplifying expressions and substituting.

  • I can use a letter to stand for an unknown number and write simple expressions (e.g. "5 more than n")
  • I can collect like terms, e.g. 3a + 2b + 5a − b
  • I can collect like terms including x and x², e.g. 2x² + 3x − x² + 4
Curriculum links (Experiences and Outcomes)
  • MTH 3-14a I can collect like algebraic terms, simplify expressions and evaluate using substitution.

Week of 30 November 2026

Algebra – collecting like terms, simplifying expressions and substituting.

  • I can simplify products, e.g. 3 × 2a, 4a × 5b, a × a
  • I can substitute positive whole numbers into expressions, e.g. find 3a + 2b when a = 4, b = 5
  • I can substitute negative numbers and evaluate formulae in words and in symbols
Curriculum links (Experiences and Outcomes)
  • MTH 3-14a I can collect like algebraic terms, simplify expressions and evaluate using substitution.

Week of 7 December 2026

Algebra – collecting like terms, simplifying expressions and substituting.

  • I can write and simplify expressions for perimeters of shapes
  • I can solve algebra problems and puzzles (e.g. algebra pyramids, magic squares)
Curriculum links (Experiences and Outcomes)
  • MTH 3-14a I can collect like algebraic terms, simplify expressions and evaluate using substitution.
