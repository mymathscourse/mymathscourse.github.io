Ratio

What you'll learn

Week of 25 January 2027

Ratio

  • I understand the concept of sharing evenly. I can perform calculations without a calculator (e.g. Share £42 evenly between 6 people) and with a calculator (e.g. A jug has a capacity of 5430ml. If a cup has a volume of 230ml, how many cups can be filled with one jug?)
  • I know that ratio is a way of expressing sharing unevenly.
  • I can simplify ratios and can identify that this is similar to simplifying fractions (e.g. simplify the ratio 4:12, simplify the ratio 6:36:60)
  • I can share in a ratio of two parts (e.g. share 55kg in the ratio 4:7)
  • I can share in a ratio of three parts (e.g. share £70 in the ratio 1:2:4)
  • I can carry out ratio calculations in a context (e.g. the ratio of sheep: cows is 4:7. If there are 20 cows, how many sheep are there?)
Curriculum links (Experiences and Outcomes)
  • MNU 3-08a I can show how quantities that are related can be increased or decreased proportionally and apply this to solve problems in everyday contexts.

Week of 1 February 2027

Ratio

  • I can link ratios and fractions (e.g. in the ratio 2:3 the first part is 2/5 of the whole)
  • I can use direct proportion and the unitary method (e.g. scaling a recipe for 4 people up to 6)
  • I can find the best buy by comparing unit prices
