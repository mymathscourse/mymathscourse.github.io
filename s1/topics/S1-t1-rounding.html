Week of 14 September 2026

Rounding

  • Prior knowledge – rounding to the nearest 1, 10, 100, 1000. Ability to use place value charts.
  • Placing decimals on a number line where points are given (e.g. place 1.73 on the number line below)
  • Constructing own number line and placing decimals on it. (e.g. draw your own number line and place 6.23 on it)
  • Rounding to one decimal place using the number line (e.g. place 5.34 on the number line below. By identifying which end it is closest to, round 5.34 to 1 d.p.)
  • Rounding to 1 d.p. without a given number line (e.g. round 83.129 to 1 d.p.)
  • Rounding harder numbers to 1 d.p. (e.g. 67.99 to 1 d.p.)
  • Identifying/applying a general rule (4 or less, stay the same, 5 or more round up)
  • Rounding numbers to 2 d.p. (e.g. round 42.4792 to 2 d.p.)
  • Rounding numbers to 3 d.p. (e.g. round 1873.2839 to 3 d.p.)
  • By rounding to an appropriate degree of accuracy, estimate an answer to 31.93 x 43.98.
  • Rounding sensibly in context (e.g. how many 52-seater buses for 130 pupils? How much paint to buy?)
Curriculum links (Experiences and Outcomes)
  • MNU 3-01a I can round a number using an appropriate degree of accuracy, having taken into account the context of the problem.
