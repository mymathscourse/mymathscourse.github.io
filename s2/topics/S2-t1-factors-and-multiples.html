What you'll learn

Week of 31 August 2026

Factors and Multiples

  • I can list factors (using factor pairs) and multiples of a number
  • I can find the highest common factor and lowest common multiple
  • I can solve HCF/LCM problems (e.g. hot dogs in packs of 8, rolls in packs of 6)
Curriculum links (Experiences and Outcomes)
  • MTH 3-05a I have investigated strategies for identifying common multiples and common factors, explaining my ideas to others, and can apply my understanding to solve related problems.

Week of 7 September 2026

Factors and Multiples

  • I can identify prime numbers (e.g. Sieve of Eratosthenes)
  • I can write a number as a product of prime factors using a factor tree
  • I can use prime factors to find the HCF and LCM (Venn diagram method)
Curriculum links (Experiences and Outcomes)
  • MTH 3-05b I can apply my understanding of factors to investigate and identify when a number is prime.

Week of 14 September 2026

Factors and Multiples

  • I can use divisibility tests for 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 9 and 10
  • I can link the HCF to simplifying fractions and to finding a common factor (prep for factorising)
