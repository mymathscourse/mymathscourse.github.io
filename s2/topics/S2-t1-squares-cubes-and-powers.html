What you'll learn

Week of 17 August 2026

Squares, Cubes and Powers

  • I can recognise square and cube numbers and draw them as arrays/cubes
  • I can write and evaluate powers using index notation, e.g. 2⁵, 3⁴, mentally and with a calculator
  • I can find square roots and cube roots of perfect squares and cubes
Curriculum links (Experiences and Outcomes)
  • MTH 3-06a Having explored the notation and vocabulary associated with whole number powers and the advantages of writing numbers in this form, I can evaluate powers of whole numbers mentally or using technology.

Week of 24 August 2026

Squares, Cubes and Powers

  • I can use powers of 10 and link them to place value
  • I can estimate square roots between two whole numbers (e.g. √50 is between 7 and 8)
  • I can use powers in context (area of a square, volume of a cube)
  • I can write large numbers using powers of ten (introduction to scientific notation)
