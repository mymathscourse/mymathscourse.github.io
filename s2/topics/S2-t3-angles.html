Angles

What you'll learn

Week of 18 January 2027

Angles – angles at right angles, angles in a triangle

  • Prior knowledge – a right angle is 90, angles in a triangle add up to 180, angles on a straight line add up to 180, angles round a point add to 360. Ability to use a protractor to measure angles and to construct angles.
  • Finding missing angles in a right angle
  • Finding missing angles on a straight line
  • Finding missing angles in a triangle.
  • Finding missing angles in an isosceles triangle
  • Combining angles facts from straight line and triangle to find missing angles
Curriculum links (Experiences and Outcomes)
  • MTH 3-17a I can name angles and find their sizes using my knowledge of the properties of a range of 2D shapes and the angle properties associated with intersecting and parallel lines.

Week of 25 January 2027

Vertically opposite angles, angles in parallel lines.

  • Naming angles using three-letter notation (e.g. angle ABC)
  • Finding missing vertically opposite angles.
  • Finding missing angles in parallel lines
  • Angles in quadrilaterals (sum to 360°)
  • Combining angles facts to find missing angles
Curriculum links (Experiences and Outcomes)
  • MTH 3-17a I can name angles and find their sizes using my knowledge of the properties of a range of 2D shapes and the angle properties associated with intersecting and parallel lines.
