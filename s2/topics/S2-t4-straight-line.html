Straight Line

What you'll learn

Week of 24 May 2027

Straight Line

  • I can plot and read points in all four quadrants (revisit)
  • Calculating the gradient of a slope
  • Calculating gradient in context (ramps, roofs, roads) and comparing slopes
  • Calculating the gradient of a slope on a graph
  • Identifying the y-intercept from a graph
  • Writing the equation of a straight line in the form y=mx+c
  • Identifying the gradient and y-intercept when given an equation of a straight line.
  • Rearranging to get into the form y=mx+c (e.g. y − 5 = 3x)
  • When given the gradient and a set of coordinates, I can give the equation of a straight line.
Curriculum links (Experiences and Outcomes)
  • MTH 4-13b I have discussed ways to describe the slope of a line, can interpret the definition of gradient and can use it to make relevant calculations, interpreting my answer for the context of the problem.
  • MTH 4-18a I can plot and describe the position of a point on a 4-quadrant coordinate grid.

Week of 31 May 2027

Straight Line – horizontal/vertical lines and sequences

  • I can write the equation of a vertical line (x = a) and a horizontal line (y = b)
  • I can find a general formula for a pattern of diagrams (building on S2 Term 1)
  • I can use the formula to find a given term and to work backwards
  • I can link the constant difference to the gradient of a straight line
Curriculum links (Experiences and Outcomes)
  • MTH 4-13c Having investigated the pattern of the coordinate points lying on a horizontal or vertical line, I can describe the pattern using a simple equation.
  • MTH 4-13a Having explored how real-life situations can be modelled by number patterns, I can establish a number sequence to represent a physical or pictorial pattern, determine a general formula to describe the sequence, then use it to make evaluations and solve related problems.

Week of 7 June 2027

Straight Line – plotting from a formula

  • I can complete a table of values from an equation and plot the line
  • I can sketch a line from y = mx + c using the gradient and y-intercept
  • I can read values from straight-line graphs in context (conversion graphs, taxi fares, phone tariffs)
  • I can find where two lines intersect from a graph (lead-in to simultaneous equations)
Curriculum links (Experiences and Outcomes)
  • MTH 4-13d I can use a given formula to generate points lying on a straight line, plot them to create a graphical representation then use this to answer related questions.
