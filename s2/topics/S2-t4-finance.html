Finance

What you'll learn

Week of 3 May 2027

Finance – credit and debt

  • I can calculate overdrafts
  • I can calculate how much a loan will cost given a rate of interest
  • I can calculate savings rates (including compound interest)
  • I can calculate interest on credit and store cards (APR) and explain the risks of debt
  • I can calculate appreciation and depreciation using a multiplier, e.g. × 1.03³
Curriculum links (Experiences and Outcomes)
  • MNU 4-09a I can discuss and illustrate the facts I need to consider when determining what I can afford, in order to manage credit and debt and lead a responsible lifestyle.

Week of 10 May 2027

Finance – earnings and deductions

  • I can calculate wages, including overtime and 'time and a half'
  • I can calculate deductions including tax rates (in different bands)
  • I can calculate National Insurance and pension deductions
  • I can calculate commission and bonuses
  • I can read and complete a payslip to find net pay
Curriculum links (Experiences and Outcomes)
  • MNU 4-09b I can source information on earnings and deductions and use it when making calculations to determine net income.

Week of 17 May 2027

Finance – personal finance products

  • I can complete 'best buy' calculations.
  • I can select a product that best meets my needs
  • Hire purchase agreements
  • I can compare contracts and tariffs (e.g. mobile phones, energy, insurance)
  • I can convert between currencies (revisit)
Curriculum links (Experiences and Outcomes)
  • MNU 4-09c I can research, compare and contrast a range of personal finance products and, after making calculations, explain my preferred choices.
