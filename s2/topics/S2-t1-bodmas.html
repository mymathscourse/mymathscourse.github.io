Week of 10 August 2026

BODMAS

  • Recap BODMAS with whole numbers, negatives and brackets, e.g. 18 − 2 × (−3)
  • BODMAS with squares and division lines, e.g. 2 + 3², (12 + 8) ÷ 4 written as a fraction
  • Applying BODMAS to algebraic terms, e.g. simplify 2a + 3 × 4a, 12a ÷ 3 + a
  • Substituting in the correct order, e.g. find 3x² when x = 2 (not 6²), find 2(a + b) when a = 3, b = −1
  • Writing expressions with brackets from words, e.g. "add 4 to n, then multiply by 3" is 3(n + 4)
Curriculum links (Experiences and Outcomes)
  • MTH 4-03b I have investigated how introducing brackets to an expression can change the emphasis and can demonstrate my understanding by using the correct order of operations when carrying out calculations.
  • MTH 3-14a I can collect like algebraic terms, simplify expressions and evaluate using substitution.
