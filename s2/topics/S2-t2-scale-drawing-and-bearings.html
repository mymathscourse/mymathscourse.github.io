Week of 9 November 2026

Scale Drawing and Bearings

  • I can use a scale to find real distances from a map or plan (e.g. 1 cm : 2 km, 1 : 50 000)
  • I can make a scale drawing of a room or plan
  • I can enlarge and reduce shapes using a scale factor

Curriculum links (Experiences and Outcomes)
  • MTH 3-17b Having investigated navigation in the world, I can apply my understanding of bearings and scale to interpret maps and plans and create accurate plans, and scale drawings of routes and journeys.
  • MTH 3-17c I can apply my understanding of scale when enlarging or reducing pictures and shapes, using different methods, including technology.
