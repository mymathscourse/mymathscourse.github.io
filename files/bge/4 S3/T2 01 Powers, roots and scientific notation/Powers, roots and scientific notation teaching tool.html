Working

    Tips: press Next step (or the space bar / right arrow) to build the working one step at a time, and Back (left arrow) to go back. Powers of whole numbers up to 12 squared and 5 cubed are drawn on squared grids. In scientific notation the decimal point stays still and the digits move, like the place value charts in the workbooklet.
