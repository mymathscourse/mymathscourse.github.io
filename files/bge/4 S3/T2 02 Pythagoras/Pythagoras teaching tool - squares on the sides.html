      Tips: press Next step (or the space bar / right arrow) to build the diagram one step at a time. Blue and red are the two shorter sides and the squares drawn on them. Purple is the hypotenuse: its square has the same area as the blue and red squares together. When the sides are whole numbers up to 20, the squares are split into 1 by 1 squares you can count.
