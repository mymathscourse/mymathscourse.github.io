        Type a number, then multiply or divide. Watch the digits move. The decimal point stays where it is.

        Dashed tiles are placeholder zeros. HTh = hundred thousands, TTh = ten thousands, Th = thousands, H = hundreds, T = tens, O = ones, t = tenths, h = hundredths, th = thousandths, tth = ten-thousandths.
