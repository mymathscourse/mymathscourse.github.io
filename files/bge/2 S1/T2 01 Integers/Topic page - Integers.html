          Week 2

          Why is negative × negative positive?

          Copy and continue the pattern. What happens to the answer each time?

          3 × (−2) = −6
          2 × (−2) = −4
          1 × (−2) = −2
          0 × (−2) = 0
          (−1) × (−2) = ?
          (−2) × (−2) = ?

          Extra: find all the pairs of integers that multiply to make −12.

          AnswerThe answer goes up by 2 each time, so (−1) × (−2) = 2, (−2) × (−2) = 4, (−3) × (−2) = 6. Pairs for −12: 1 and −12, −1 and 12, 2 and −6, −2 and 6, 3 and −4, −3 and 4.
