    Tips: press Next step (or the space bar / right arrow) to build the working one step at a time, and the left arrow to go back. Coordinates are (x, y): along the corridor, then up the stairs. Change Each square is worth to practise fractional and decimal coordinates; you can type numbers like 1.5, 1½, 1 1/2 or 3/2. Type corners in brackets, e.g. (1, 1) (3, 1) (1, 4).
