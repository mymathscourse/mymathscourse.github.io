          Rich tasks

          Week 1

          Same or different?

          Draw an area model for {2/3} of {3/4}. Then draw one for {3/4} of {2/3}.

          What do you notice? Will this always happen? Explain why using your diagrams.

          AnswerBoth are {6/12} = {1/2}. The diagrams are the same square turned round: the columns and rows swap, but the overlap has the same number of parts. Multiplying in either order always gives the same answer.
          Week 1

          Missing digits

          Use four different digits from 1 to 9 to fill the boxes. Both fractions must be less than 1.

          × = {1/2}

          There are only two ways (not counting swapping the fractions round). Can you find both?

          Answer{2/3} × {6/8} = {12/24} = {1/2} and {3/4} × {6/9} = {18/36} = {1/2}.
          Week 2

          Bigger or smaller?

          Without working them out, sort these into "answer bigger than 12" and "answer smaller than 12":

          12 × {3/4},   12 ÷ {3/4},   12 × {1_1/2},   12 ÷ {1_1/2}

          Explain your choices, then work them out to check.

          AnswerBigger: 12 ÷ {3/4} = 16 and 12 × {1_1/2} = 18. Smaller: 12 × {3/4} = 9 and 12 ÷ {1_1/2} = 8. Multiplying by a number less than 1 or dividing by a number bigger than 1 makes the answer smaller.
          Week 3

          Morag's money

          Morag spends {1/4} of her money on a ferry ticket to Tarbert. She spends {2/3} of what is left on food. She has £12 left.

          How much money did she start with? Draw a bar or area model to help.

          Answer£48. After the ticket, {3/4} is left. Food is {2/3} of {3/4} = {1/2} of her money. So {1/4} + {1/2} = {3/4} is spent and {1/4} is left. {1/4} is £12, so she started with 4 × £12 = £48.
          Challenge

          UKMT-style

          1. Work out (1 − {1/2}) × (1 − {1/3}) × (1 − {1/4}) × … × (1 − {1/10}).

          2. How many pieces {3/8} m long can be cut from a plank {4_1/2} m long?

          Answer1. {1/10}. It is {1/2} × {2/3} × {3/4} × … × {9/10}: every numerator cancels with the denominator before it. 2. 12 pieces: {9/2} ÷ {3/8} = {9/2} × {8/3} = {72/6} = 12.
          Explain it

          Why does keep, change, flip work?

          3 ÷ {1/4} means "how many quarters fit into 3?" Draw it.

          Now explain why 3 ÷ {1/4} gives the same answer as 3 × 4. What about 3 ÷ {3/4}?

          AnswerEach whole holds 4 quarters, so 3 wholes hold 3 × 4 = 12 quarters. Dividing by {1/4} is the same as multiplying by 4, which is {4/1}: the flipped fraction. For 3 ÷ {3/4}, the 12 quarters make groups of 3, so 12 ÷ 3 = 4. That is 3 × {4/3} = 4.

          Fraction talks

          What fraction is shaded? How do you know?

          Look at the picture on your own first, then share your thinking. There is often more than one way to see it. Try to write what you see as a multiplication, e.g. “half of a quarter” is {1/2} × {1/4}.

          Talk 1

          Halves and quarters

          1. What fraction is the small shaded piece? Write it as a multiplication.
          2. What fraction of the whole square is shaded?
          3. What fraction is not shaded?
          Answer1. {1/4} of {1/2} = {1/4} × {1/2} = {1/8}. 2. {1/8} + {1/4} = {1/8} + {2/8} = {3/8}. 3. {5/8}.
          Talk 2

          Thirds

          1. What fraction is shaded in the right-hand column? Write it as a multiplication.
          2. What fraction of the whole square is shaded?
          3. How many of the smallest pieces would fit into the whole square? Into the shaded part?
          Answer1. {3/4} of {1/3} = {3/4} × {1/3} = {3/12} = {1/4}. 2. {1/3} + {1/4} = {4/12} + {3/12} = {7/12}. 3. Each small piece is {1/12}, so 12 fit into the whole (1 ÷ {1/12} = 12) and 7 fit into the shaded part.
          Talk 3

          Triangle and square

          1. What fraction is the shaded triangle? Write it as a multiplication.
          2. What fraction is the small shaded square?
          3. What fraction of the whole square is shaded altogether?
          Answer1. {1/2} of {1/4} = {1/8}. 2. {1/4} of {1/4} = {1/16}. 3. {1/8} + {1/16} = {2/16} + {1/16} = {3/16}.
          Talk 4

          The garden

          1. What fraction is shaded? Write it as a multiplication.
          2. The square is a garden with an area of 20 m². What area is shaded?
          3. What area is not shaded?
          Answer1. {1/2} of {3/5} = {1/2} × {3/5} = {3/10}. 2. {3/10} × 20 = 6 m². 3. 20 − 6 = 14 m² (or {7/10} × 20 = 14 m²).
