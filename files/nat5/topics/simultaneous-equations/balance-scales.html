Simultaneous equations on the scales

Each equation is a balanced set of scales. Whatever you do to one side, do to the other and it stays balanced. Click two tiles to cancel them: x and a −x balloon on the same side make zero, and the same tile on both sides can be taken off both. Put two scales together (A + B or A − B) to knock out a letter. Balloons are negative: they pull the pan up.

Put two scales together

Taking away B means every tile from B goes in upside down: weights become balloons and balloons become weights.
