Quadratic equations

What you'll learn

Week of 18 January 2027

Quadratics by factorizing

  • Rearranging so the equation equals zero
  • Factorise, then set each bracket equal to zero
  • All four factorising types: common factor, DoTs, monic, non-monic
  • Checking roots by substitution

Dynamic Maths: R 1.3 – Quadratic equation by factorising

Week of 25 January 2027

Quadratic formula

  • Identifying a, b and c (including negative values)
  • Substituting carefully – brackets around negatives
  • Rounding to the required number of decimal places
  • Knowing when to use it: “give your answer to 1 / 2 decimal places”

Dynamic Maths: R 1.3 – Quadratic formula

Week of 1 February 2027

Quadratics in context

  • Forming a quadratic equation from an area or shape problem
  • Solving and rejecting impossible answers (e.g. a negative length)
  • Intersection of a line and a parabola
  • Past exam questions

Dynamic Maths: R 1.3 Quadratic equations

Week of 8 February 2027

Discriminant

  • The discriminant b² − 4ac
  • Nature of roots: real and distinct, real and equal, no real roots
  • Linking the discriminant to the graph (how many times it crosses the x-axis)
  • Finding an unknown e.g. find k so the equation has equal roots
