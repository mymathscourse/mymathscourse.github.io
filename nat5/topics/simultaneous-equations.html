Notes and practice

  • PDFSimultaneous equations: pupil notes

    6 pages, black and white: key facts, Examples 1–17 (graphical, elimination, substitution, forming equations) each with a “Your turn”, a test-yourself table and answers.

  • InteractiveSimultaneous equations: pupil topic page

    For pupils: the nine I can steps (1.1 to 5.2), each with worked examples, new questions every time, a scaffold (graph grid, the balance scales, or step-by-step hints), flashcards and links. Whole topic: mixed check in, word scramble and rich tasks.

  • InteractiveSimultaneous equations on the scales

    Each equation is a set of balance scales with x, y and number tiles. Multiply, put two scales together (A + B or A − B), click tiles to cancel them, share to find x, then put x back in to find y.

  • InteractiveSimultaneous equations explorer

    Tabs: Graphically · Elimination · Substitution · In context · Mixed practice. Worked examples revealed step by step, then practice questions under every tab (C, A and extension, calculator or non-calculator) with worked solutions.
