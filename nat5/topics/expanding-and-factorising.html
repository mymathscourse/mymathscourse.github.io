National 5

Expanding, factorising and completing the square

What you'll learn

Week of 13 April 2026

Expanding brackets

  • Multiplying algebraic terms e.g. 3x × (−2x) = −6x²
  • Expanding a single bracket, including negatives outside e.g. −3(2x − 5)
  • Expanding and simplifying two single brackets e.g. 4(x + 2) − 3(x − 1)
  • Expanding double brackets with the grid method, then FOIL
  • Expanding a squared bracket e.g. (x − 4)², and why it is not x² + 16

Dynamic Maths: E&F 1.2 Brackets and Factorising

Week of 20 April 2026

Expanding brackets with trinomials

  • Review expanding double brackets
  • Expanding a bracket × trinomial e.g. (x + 2)(x² − 3x + 1)
  • Collecting like terms carefully after expanding
  • Expanding and simplifying in area / perimeter contexts

Dynamic Maths: E&F 1.2 Brackets and Factorising

Week of 27 April 2026

Factorising with a common factor

  • Highest common factor of numbers, then of terms e.g. HCF of 6x² and 9x
  • Factorising by taking out a common factor e.g. 12x − 18 = 6(2x − 3)
  • Common factors that include a letter e.g. 5x² + 15x
  • Checking by expanding back out

Dynamic Maths: E&F 1.2 Brackets and Factorising

Week of 4 May 2026

Factorising Monic Quadratics

  • Link to expanding: factorising as the reverse of (x + a)(x + b)
  • Factor pairs of c that add to give b
  • Positive b and c, e.g. x² + 7x + 12
  • Negative b, positive c, e.g. x² − 9x + 14
  • Negative c, e.g. x² + 2x − 15 and x² − 3x − 10
  • Check every answer by expanding

Dynamic Maths: E&F 1.2 Brackets and Factorising

Week of 18 May 2026

Factorising non-monic quadratics

  • Review monic factorising
  • Factor pairs of both the x² coefficient and the constant
  • Trial and check (or grid / grouping method) e.g. 2x² + 7x + 3
  • Negative constants e.g. 3x² − 5x − 2
  • Check by expanding

Dynamic Maths: E&F 1.2 Brackets and Factorising

Week of 25 May 2026

Factorising DoTs

  • Recognising square numbers and square terms (x², 9y², 25)
  • Discover the pattern by expanding (x − 3)(x + 3)
  • Factorising a² − b² = (a − b)(a + b)
  • With coefficients e.g. 16x² − 49y²
  • Spotting when DoTs applies: two terms, a minus sign, both squares

Dynamic Maths: E&F 1.2 Brackets and Factorising

Week of 1 June 2026

Factorising with a combination of common factor, DoTs/Trinomial

  • Always look for a common factor first – what “factorise fully” means
  • Common factor then DoTs e.g. 2x² − 18
  • Common factor then trinomial e.g. 3x² + 6x − 24
  • Mixed practice: deciding which method(s) to use

Week of 10 August 2026

Review of Expanding and Factorising algebraic expressions

  • Quick-fire recap of expanding: single, double, squared and trinomial brackets
  • Recap of the four factorising types: common factor, DoTs, monic, non-monic
  • Retrieval starters and mixed practice
  • Past exam questions on expanding and factorising

Week of 17 August 2026

Rationalising the denominator and Completing the Square

  • Multiplying a surd by itself e.g. 5×5=5
  • Making equivalent fractions using surds e.g. 35=3555
  • Simplifying fractions with surds
  • Expanding a bracket squared e.g. x-52
  • Comparing two expressions with each other e.g. x2+4x-10=x2+4x-6-4
