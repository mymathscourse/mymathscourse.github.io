National 5

Trig graphs, equations and identities

What you'll learn

Week of 1 March 2027

Trig graphs

  • Key features of y = sin x, y = cos x and y = tan x
  • Amplitude: y = a sin x
  • Frequency: y = sin bx
  • Vertical shift: y = sin x + c, and phase angle: y = sin(x − p)
  • Identifying the equation from a graph

Dynamic Maths: R 1.5 Further Trigonometry (trig graphs)

Week of 8 March 2027

Trig equations

  • Solving sin x = k, cos x = k, tan x = k for 0 ≤ x < 360
  • Using the CAST diagram / graph symmetry to find the second solution
  • Rearranging first e.g. 5 cos x + 2 = 0
  • Finding points of intersection of a trig graph and a line

Dynamic Maths: R 1.5 Further Trigonometry (trig equations)

Week of 15 March 2027

Trig identities

  • sin²x + cos²x = 1
  • tan x = sin x / cos x
  • Simplifying trig expressions
  • Proving identities: work from one side only
