Higher Maths

This page shows what the class is learning each week and what is coming next. Dates can move a little if a class needs more time on a topic.

For the whole course

  • PDFDecember prelim preparation pack

    20 pages: confidence checklist, 12 topic sections matching the prelim, mixed Paper 1 and Paper 2 style questions with marks, and answers with key working.

  • PDFDecember prelim revision pack (facts, plan and resources)

    10 pages: topic checklist linked to the practice prelim questions, how long and how to revise (about 3–4 hours a week for the last 3 weeks), a 3-week planner, 115 cover-and-test key facts on the 12 prelim topics, and links to highermathematics.co.uk and DynamicMaths.

  • PDFFormulae to learn

    One page of the formulae not on the exam formulae list: straight line, algebra, logs and trig.

  • PDFRevision cards: pupil booklet

    7 pages: the same 113 cards by topic, questions on the left and answers on the right, with a tick box for each. Pupils cover or fold the answers to test themselves.

  • PDFFormulae list: pupil copy

    The same formulae as the SQA exam list, typeset for the course website. Source: Revision/formulae-list-pupil.tex.
